## A little about me

I'm from Nepal, with a background in Agriculture and Soil Science. I use this space to share code, small projects, experiments, and things I'm learning. Most of my work sits somewhere between agriculture, maps, data analysis, and automation—with machine learning mixed in.

## What I upload here

- 🗺️ **GIS & remote sensing:** ArcGIS Pro, QGIS, Google Earth Engine scripts, satellite imagery, NDVI, and spatial analysis.
- ⚙️ **GIS automation:** Python/ArcPy scripts and R workflows to make repetitive geospatial tasks easier.
- 🌱 **Agriculture data analysis:** R scripts for experimental design and agricultural data analysis, especially with `agricolae` and `doebioresearch`.
- 📊 **Data science & finance:** Python notebooks, statistics, time-series analysis, derivatives pricing, and machine-learning experiments.
- 🧪 **Learning projects:** Practice code, useful snippets, and experiments with tools I’m exploring.

## A few things I enjoy

- Finding ways to automate repetitive work
- Exploring maps, satellite images, and interesting datasets
- Trying new R and Python packages
- Learning about agriculture, data, and quantitative finance
- Learning new things and building small projects along the way

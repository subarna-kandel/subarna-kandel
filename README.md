## 👋 About Me

I'm from Nepal, with a background in Agriculture and Soil Science. I use this space to share code, practical projects, experiments, and things I'm learning.

My work often connects **agriculture, geospatial data, data analysis, and automation**—with machine learning and quantitative finance among my other interests. I enjoy finding ways to make complex or repetitive tasks simpler with code.
